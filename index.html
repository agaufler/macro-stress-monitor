📐 Yield Curve

Treasury Yields — 3mo / 2yr / 10yr / 30yr

When short-term yields exceed long-term yields, the curve is inverted — historically the most reliable recession signal, typically 6–18 months ahead. Watch for 2yr > 10yr.

🏦 Fed Controls: Fed Funds Rate vs. T-Bills

The Fed sets the overnight rate. T-Bills (short-term, <1yr) follow it almost immediately. This is the rate you hear about at Fed press conferences. Currently — — being cut slowly after peaking near 5.5%.

📈 Market Controls: 2yr / 10yr / 30yr Treasury Yields

The market sets these based on inflation expectations, growth outlook, and supply/demand. The Fed can influence but not dictate. When 10yr > 2yr, curve is normal. When 2yr > 10yr, it’s inverted — recession signal.

📈 Total US Gross Debt Over Time ($T)

Gross federal debt — everything the US government owes, including intragovernmental debt (Social Security trust fund, etc.). Crossed $36T in 2025. Adding roughly $1T every 90–100 days.

💰 How Much Debt Is In Each Bucket?

T-Notes (2-10yr) are the biggest chunk at $14.7T. T-Bills ($5.8T) roll over constantly — refinanced at current rates every few weeks. T-Bonds $4.5T, TIPS $2.0T, FRNs $600B. Blue = Fed-influenced. Yellow/Red = market-set.

💸 National Debt

US National Debt vs. GDP (Debt-to-GDP Ratio)

Raw debt numbers are hard to interpret — $39 trillion sounds big but context matters. Debt-to-GDP above 100% means the country owes more than it produces in a year. The US crossed that threshold and hasn't looked back.
🌏 Global Risk Signals

USD/JPY — Yen Carry Trade Signal

Japan's low interest rates let traders borrow cheap yen, invest in higher-yield assets. When JPY strengthens rapidly (USD/JPY falls), the carry trade unwinds — triggering global sell-offs. Aug 2024 was a warning shot.

High-Yield Credit Spread (OAS)

The extra yield junk bonds pay over Treasuries. Spikes when credit markets smell trouble — often before stocks react. Below 3% = complacency. Above 6% = stress. Above 10% = crisis.
🌍 Foreign Holdings of US Debt

Who Holds US Treasury Debt — Top 8 Countries ($B)

Current holdings by major foreign creditors. Japan and China have both been selling Treasuries. When major holders exit, bond prices fall and yields rise — increasing US borrowing costs.

Japan vs. China — Treasury Holdings Over Time ($B)

Japan overtook China as the #1 holder. Both are reducing exposure. China was at $1.03T in Jan 2022 — now at $859B. A continued exit = upward pressure on US yields.

Trend Since Jan 2022 (Change in $B)

Net change in holdings from Jan 2022 to latest. Green = buying. Red = selling. The selling trend from Japan and China is structural, not a blip.
🖨️ Money Supply

M2 Money Supply ($B)

Total money in circulation. The Fed printed ~$5 trillion during COVID (2020–2022), inflating asset prices. It's now slowly contracting. M2 growth correlates with stock prices ~6 months later — watch the trend.
